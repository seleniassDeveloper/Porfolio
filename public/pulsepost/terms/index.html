Operated by Selenia Sanchez · Effective 21 November 2026 · This page is public and does not require an account.

These terms describe PulsePost, a personal, private tool operated by Selenia Sanchez. The tool is used only by the operator to manage her own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, does not accept public registration, is not sold, and has no subscriptions, billing, or commercial plans.

The tool

  • Connects the operator's own accounts through each network's official OAuth flow and publishes or schedules her content through official APIs.
  • Reads basic metrics of the operator's own posts.
  • Sends automatic keyword replies on the operator's own Instagram professional accounts: when someone comments a keyword on one of her posts, or sends her a direct message with a keyword, the tool replies with a predefined message. Automatic messages are labeled as automatic.

The tool never asks for social network passwords.

Access

Access is limited to the operator. There is no public sign-up, no customer accounts, and no invitation of third-party users.

Automatic replies

  • The tool only replies to people who first comment on the operator's posts or send her a direct message. It never starts a conversation with someone who has not written first.
  • It answers each comment or message at most once, and only within the time limits Instagram allows.
  • Anyone can stop the conversation at any time by not replying, and can ask for their data to be deleted as described in the Privacy Policy.

Accounts and content

  • Only accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage are connected.
  • The operator is responsible for the content published and the messages sent, and for complying with each network's rules and with the law.
  • The tool must not be used for spam, impersonation, infringement of third-party rights, or any unlawful activity, and must not be used to bypass a network's limits.

Third-party platforms

Connecting a network also means accepting that network's terms. For Instagram and Facebook, that includes Instagram's Terms of Use and Meta's policies. Use of information received from Meta APIs complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

Networks may change, limit, or withdraw their APIs at any time. A post or reply may fail or be delayed for reasons outside the operator's control. The tool shows the status of each publication and each automatic reply.

Content

The operator keeps all rights to her content. The tool processes that content only to store it, schedule it, and send it to the networks she chooses.

Stopping use and deleting data

The operator can disconnect any account or stop using the tool at any time. See the Privacy Policy for how stored data is deleted.
