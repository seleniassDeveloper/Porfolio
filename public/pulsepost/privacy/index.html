PulsePost

Privacy Policy

Operated by Selenia Sanchez · Effective 10 November 2026 · This page is public and does not require an account.

PulsePost is a personal, private tool operated by Selenia Sanchez. It is used only by the operator to manage her own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, does not accept public registration, is not sold, and has no subscriptions or billing.

This policy explains what data the tool processes. Contact: ssseleniasanchez@gmail.com.

What the tool does

  • Connects the operator's own accounts through each network's official OAuth flow and publishes or schedules the operator's content through official APIs.
  • Reads basic metrics (likes and comments) of the operator's own posts.
  • Sends automatic keyword replies on the operator's own Instagram professional accounts: when someone comments a keyword on one of her posts, or sends her a direct message with a keyword, the tool replies with a predefined message (for example, a link to a free checklist or guide). Automatic messages are labeled as automatic.

The tool never asks for or stores social network passwords.

Data the tool processes

  • Operator sign-in: only the operator can sign in, with a password configured on the server. There are no other user accounts.
  • Connected accounts: the identifiers each network provides through OAuth (account ID, username, profile picture URL, permissions granted).
  • Authorizations (tokens): access tokens issued by each network, encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage, never shown in the browser, and erased when the account is disconnected.
  • Content and publishing: the text, schedule, media and result (post ID and URL) of the operator's posts.
  • Comments and direct messages received by the operator's Instagram accounts (only for automatic replies): the Instagram-scoped ID and username of the person, the text of the comment or message, its date, and whether that person follows the operator's account. The tool reads whether the person follows the account only after that person has sent a direct message. This data is kept as an event log so the operator can see what was answered and so the same message is never answered twice.

Instagram and Meta permissions

  • instagram_business_basic: identify the connected account.
  • instagram_business_content_publish: publish the operator's content.
  • instagram_business_manage_insights: read metrics of the operator's posts.
  • instagram_business_manage_comments: receive comments on the operator's posts and reply to them.
  • instagram_business_manage_messages: receive direct messages sent to the operator's accounts and send the automatic reply.

Data received from Meta APIs is used only to operate the operator's own accounts. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles of people. Use of information received from Meta APIs complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

Service providers

The tool runs on Render (application server), Neon (PostgreSQL database) and Vercel (web interface). These providers store data only to run the tool.

Data deletion

Anyone can ask for their data to be deleted:

  1. Write to ssseleniasanchez@gmail.com with your Instagram username and the subject "Data deletion".
  2. The operator deletes every comment, message and event linked to that username from the tool and confirms by email within 30 days.

To remove the tool's access to an Instagram account, disconnect it in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Stored tokens and account data for that connection are then erased.

Children

This tool is not offered to the public and is not directed at children.
